- Roman to Integer, Minecraft edition. LeetCode problem number thirteen, an easy one with a twist.
- Our row of blocks spells a Roman numeral. Each block is a symbol with a value.
- Turn the whole row into a normal number. Here, M C M X C I V should give nineteen ninety four.
- The first idea is to add up every symbol, from left to right.
- One thousand, plus one hundred, plus one thousand, plus ten, and so on.
- That gives two thousand two hundred sixteen. Wrong!
- Because Romans also subtract. C before M means nine hundred, not eleven hundred.
- Here's the trick. Look one block ahead. If the next symbol is bigger, this one is subtracted.
- Otherwise, it's added. One comparison per block, that's all.
- So we walk the row once, and our experience level keeps the running total.
- M is followed by C, which is smaller. Add one thousand.
- C is followed by M, which is bigger. Subtract one hundred.
- M, then X. Add one thousand. We're at nineteen hundred.
- X before C. Subtract ten. C before I. Add one hundred.
- I before V. Subtract one. And the last V has nothing after it. Add five.
- Advancement made. The total is nineteen ninety four.
- We look at each symbol once, so the time is linear in the length of the string.
- And we only keep one number, the total. Constant extra memory.
- One loop, one look ahead, and a small table of values.
- Smaller before bigger means subtract. That's the whole secret of Roman numerals.
