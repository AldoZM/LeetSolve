subtitles:
- Palindrome Number, Minecraft edition. LeetCode number nine, and a perfect warm up.
- Given an integer x, return true if it reads the same forward and backward.
- One two one is a palindrome. Minus one two one is not, because the minus sign only sits on the left.
- The easy way is to turn the number into a string, and compare it with its reverse.
- It works, but the follow up asks us to solve it without converting to a string.
- We could reverse the whole number with math, but the reversed number could overflow.
- The trick is to reverse only half of the digits.
- Pop digits from the end of x and push them onto a second number, until that number catches up with x.
- Then the two halves must match.
- Let's check one two three two one. The reversed half starts at zero.
- Pop one. x is one two three two, and the half is one.
- Pop two. x is one two three, and the half is twelve.
- Pop three. x is twelve, and the half is one hundred twenty three. Now the half is bigger, so we stop.
- The length is odd, so the middle digit, three, sits at the end of the half. Divide it by ten.
- Twelve equals twelve. Advancement made. It's a palindrome!
- We only touch half of the digits, so the time is log of x.
- No string and no array, just two numbers. Constant space.
- Negative numbers and numbers ending in zero fail right away. The rest, we fold in half.
- Reverse only half, and meet in the middle.
